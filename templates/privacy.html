Privacy

How we use your information

Smart Gifting follows Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Sender: your name and mobile number, for your receipt and for refunds.
  • Recipient: their mobile number, so we can send them the gift, and the message you write.
  • Payments: the amount, the payment method and Pesapal's payment reference. Your Mobile Money PIN and card number go to Pesapal only. We never see them.
  • Face check (only if the sender turns it on): a faceprint, which is a list of numbers made from the recipient's face, not the photo. It is stored only with the recipient's consent and deleted when the gift is collected.
  • Pickups: a record of each code check at the store (time, store and result), to stop fraud.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not keep face photos.
  • We share details only with the store that hands over the gift and with Pesapal to process the payment.

Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you signed in and to protect forms. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
